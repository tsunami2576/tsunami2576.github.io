01 · About01 · 关于

About Me关于我

A few words about who I am and what I care about. 用几句话，快速认识我。

I am a second-year PhD student in the Department of Chemistry at Tsinghua University, working at the interface of large language models and organic synthesis in Prof. Sanzhong Luo's group. My goal is to turn unstructured chemical literature into structured, searchable reaction knowledge — and to build tools that chemists actually enjoy using.

我是清华大学化学系博士二年级学生，在罗三中教授课题组从事大语言模型与有机合成交叉方向的研究。我的目标是把非结构化的化学文献变成结构化、可检索的反应知识，并做出化学家们真正愿意用的工具。

My recent work includes ReactionSeek (Nat. Commun. 2026), a framework coupling LLMs with cheminformatics for literature data mining, and its successor ReactionSeek 2.0, currently under review at CCS Chemistry. Beyond research, I am a photography enthusiast, a gamer, and a traveler.

近期工作包括 ReactionSeek（Nat. Commun. 2026）——将大语言模型与化学信息学工具结合的文献数据挖掘框架——以及它的继任者 ReactionSeek 2.0（投稿于 CCS Chemistry）。科研之外，我是摄影、游戏与旅行爱好者。
